# Phase 58 Report

The pooled-seven noisy scaffold rule was applied unchanged to an adversarial sign-break family on benchmark I.

Results at γ = 0.30:
- base-family R² ≈ 0.9808
- adversarial-family R² ≈ 0.2409
- combined R² ≈ 0.4661
- adversarial-family sign agreement ≈ 0.8333
- combined sign agreement ≈ 0.9167

Interpretation:
This is not a collapse of the noisy scaffold layer, but it is a real failure boundary for the uncorrected rule.
