# Phase 41 Report

A single pooled positive noisy scaffold rule was derived from the train rows of benchmarks C and G and then evaluated on held-out families of both benchmarks.

## Switch slice (`γ = 0.30`)
- benchmark C held-out combined: `R² ≈ 0.9412`
- benchmark C held-out new-family: `R² ≈ 0.9269`
- benchmark G held-out combined: `R² ≈ 0.9277`
- benchmark G held-out new-family: `R² ≈ 0.8947`
- pooled held-out combined across both: `R² ≈ 0.9380`
- pooled held-out new-family across both: `R² ≈ 0.9191`
- pooled combined correlation: `≈ 0.9788`
- sign agreement: `1.0`

## Read
This is stronger than a one-benchmark result and stronger than one-way transfer alone. Within the current designed scaffold family, the noisy extension now supports a **shared pooled scaffold rule**.
