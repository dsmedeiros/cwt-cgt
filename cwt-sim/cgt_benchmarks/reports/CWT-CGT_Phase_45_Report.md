At the switch slice `γ = 0.30`, the broadened pooled four-positive noisy scaffold rule gives:

- benchmark C held-out combined: `R² ≈ 0.9054`
- benchmark G held-out combined: `R² ≈ 0.9606`
- benchmark H held-out combined: `R² ≈ 0.9915`
- benchmark I held-out combined: `R² ≈ 0.9948`
- pooled held-out combined across C+G+H+I: `R² ≈ 0.9779`
- pooled combined correlation: `≈ 0.9908`
- pooled sign agreement: `≈ 0.9926`

Interpretation:
- the broadened pooled rule remains strong on the pooled held-out regular families,
- it is weaker on benchmark C than some benchmark-specific noisy rules,
- but stronger as a **shared scaffold-level rule** across multiple positive noisy benchmarks.
