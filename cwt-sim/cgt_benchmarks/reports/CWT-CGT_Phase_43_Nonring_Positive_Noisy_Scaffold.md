# Phase 43 — Non-Ring Positive Noisy Scaffold Benchmark

Goal: test whether the pooled positive-noisy scaffold rule survives transfer to a **genuinely non-ring** positive scaffold benchmark.

Benchmark I is a five-node skew ladder / non-ring scaffold. It uses the same train/held-out loop-family split as the earlier positive noisy scaffold benchmarks, but changes the underlying scaffold geometry away from the ring family.

Acceptance criterion:
- strong held-out combined fit at the switch slice,
- correct sign agreement,
- no benchmark-specific rule refit.
