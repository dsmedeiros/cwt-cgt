# CWT-CGT Theory — Hardened v44

## Current accepted structure

The theory remains organized as a layered benchmarked framework:

1. **Coherent/passive branch-resolved core**  
   Local graph dynamics settle onto branch-resolved states. Geometry over exogenous control space predicts sensitivity and orientation-dependent loop response on trusted coherent patches.

2. **Explicit regime separation**  
   Smooth coherent pumping, mixed/noisy response, topological auxiliary behavior, and R4 branch switching are treated as distinct lanes rather than one merged claim.

3. **Noisy scaffold layer**  
   The noisy extension is represented by a pooled generator-derived scaffold rule with local superoperator channels, compactness/area/tensor/covariance structure, and loop-side corrections.

## What changed in v44

This phase adds a fifth structurally different positive noisy scaffold benchmark, **benchmark J (bowtie-chain)**, evaluated under the unchanged pooled-four-positive noisy rule from C+G+H+I. It also adds a harder perturbation-family stress test on J under the same unchanged rule.

At the switch slice \(\gamma = 0.30\):

- Benchmark J train \(R^2\): **0.9729**
- Benchmark J held-out base \(R^2\): **0.9818**
- Benchmark J held-out new \(R^2\): **0.9733**
- Benchmark J held-out combined \(R^2\): **0.9769**
- Benchmark J stronger-family held-out combined \(R^2\): **0.9277**
- Benchmark J stronger-family sign agreement: **1.0**

## Interpretation

The noisy scaffold layer now has positive support on five designed scaffold benchmarks:
**C, G, H, I, J**.

The pooled rule itself is still trained on **C+G+H+I** only. J therefore functions as a real transfer test inside the scaffold program rather than an in-sample confirmation.

The harder perturbation family on J remains supportive but less clean than the regular held-out families. This strengthens the theory while also sharpening its current boundary: the noisy layer transfers across multiple structurally different scaffold cases, but performance decays under deliberately harsher perturbations.

## Current boundaries

- This is still **scaffold evidence**, not external empirical validation.
- The coherent/passive branch-resolved core remains stronger than the noisy extension.
- The noisy extension is increasingly structured and increasingly transferred, but it is still not the final microscopic theory.
