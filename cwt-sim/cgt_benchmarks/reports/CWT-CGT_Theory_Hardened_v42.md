# CWT-CGT Theory Hardened v42

The theory is now best understood as a **layered benchmarked framework** with four distinct layers:

1. a **coherent, branch-resolved passive core**,
2. an explicit **R4 branch-switching lane**,
3. an auxiliary **periodic/gapped topology lane**,
4. a **noisy scaffold lane** built from generator-derived local structure and tested on held-out loop families.

## What changed in this revision
This revision adds a genuinely **non-ring positive noisy scaffold benchmark** and then stress-tests it with a **stronger perturbation family** under the unchanged pooled noisy scaffold rule.

The main update is that the noisy scaffold layer now transfers to:
- ring-like positive scaffold benchmarks C, G, and H,
- a **non-ring ladder benchmark I**,
- and a stronger family of perturbations on benchmark I.

## Current boundary
This remains **scaffold evidence**, not external empirical validation. The noisy layer is better supported than before, but it is still not a final microscopic theory.

## Current verdict
The noisy scaffold layer is now supported across multiple positive scaffold benchmarks, including one non-ring case, with good held-out-family transfer and correct sign behavior.
