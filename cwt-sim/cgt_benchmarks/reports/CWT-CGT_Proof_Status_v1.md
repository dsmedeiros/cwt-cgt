# CWT-CGT Proof Status v1

Date: 2026-08-15

This document is the current interpretive authority for claim status. It supersedes conflicting interpretations in all earlier theory, status, hardening, acceptance, readiness, and phase reports. Their historical bodies and archived numbers remain intact, apart from explicit correction/supersession banners; their reported numbers retain only the provenance tier justified below.

## Bottom line

The repository contains useful analytic identities and synthetic calibration machinery. A new internal proof program establishes a finite-dimensional uniform-contraction response theorem and an exact arbitrary-one-form realizability/no-go theorem; the earlier benchmark-C fixed-tick result is a narrow linear-contracting corollary. The no-go result proves that contraction and smoothness alone cannot make projective CGT curvature predict response curvature. These results are `PASS_INTERNAL_ANALYTIC` / `NO_EMPIRICAL_EVIDENCE` and do not prove the central empirical claim that CGT flux independently predicts a nonzero orientation-odd CWT readout on external observations. In the benchmark-C harness, "independent response" means that response is calculated without geometry/orientation inputs; it does not mean independent empirical validation. Its local `F_R/Omega` comparison has no predictive content because pointwise proportionality of nonzero 2-forms in 2D is algebraic. A metadata-only active-loop design template records stricter external confirmation requirements, including the distinction between generic equilibrium-reset and separately justified periodic/endpoint-flat remainder rates, but it is `BLOCKED_NO_SUBSTRATE`, has no outcome loader or confirmation command, and contributes no evidence. A post-hoc OEDI IEEE123 reconstruction is reproducible from a pinned official test-system source consistent with the archived vector, but it refutes rather than supports the old structural/sign-boundary interpretation and cannot prove the original run's source revision/parser. The separately frozen same-package passive diagnostic was executed after exact-digest approval: 77/77 admitted files passed QC, but the 61-profile primary result failed (`T=0.013066368743938832<0.10`, one-sided conditional bus-bundle QAP `p=0.39696`, 99% Clopper-Pearson Monte Carlo interval for the permutation-tail probability `[0.39296862162223856,0.4009491374352013]`; 99,999 draws, no extension), with leave-one and lateral sign instability. It fails to reject the conditional random-label null and supplies no support for that auxiliary locality diagnostic. This is neither proof of exact absence nor validation/falsification of CGT/CWT, and it is not active-loop, ridge, topology, noise, physical/field, independent-replication, population, or generalization evidence. The current theory remains **needs revision / proof incomplete**.

## Provenance tiers

- **Tier A — analytic/executable:** equations are tied to implementation and focused tests recompute the result.
- **Tier B — internal synthetic:** a runner generates states/results from a declared synthetic benchmark; useful for construction and falsification, not external validation.
- **Tier C — static/adaptive summary:** checked-in JSON or a loader reproduces reported values without recomputing raw observations; descriptive only.
- **Tier D — provenance-locked retrospective external test-system reconstruction:** pinned public-source bytes, parser, checksums, and one-command reconstruction reproduce an archived vector after outcomes were known. This establishes present reproducibility only, not original historical provenance, field-observation status, prospective confirmation, independence, or theory support.
- **Tier E — unaudited derived external summary / endpoint metadata:** narrative/derived metrics or endpoint metadata without the raw payload, checksums, and one-command reproduction needed for an auditable result.
