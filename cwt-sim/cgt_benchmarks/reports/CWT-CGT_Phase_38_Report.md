# Phase 38 Report

## Summary

Phase 38 replaces the fixed compactness exponent in the Phase 37 lower-order area channel with a local superoperator-geometry compactness share derived from `share_geometry / local_area_share`.

## Switch-slice metrics at `γ = 0.30`

- Phase 37 held-out new-family `R² ≈ 0.9318`
- Phase 38 held-out new-family `R² ≈ 0.9321`
- Phase 37 held-out combined `R² ≈ 0.9743`
- Phase 38 held-out combined `R² ≈ 0.9744`
- combined correlation `≈ 0.9893`
- sign agreement `= 1.0`

## Interpretation

This is a small but real improvement over Phase 37 on both the held-out new-family slice and the held-out combined slice. The phase is best understood as a **derivational tightening with a mild accuracy gain**.
